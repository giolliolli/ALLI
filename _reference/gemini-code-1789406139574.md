# Ultraviolhat — Style Reference
> artisanal riviera crochet atelier — a quiet-luxury coastal sanctuary where crisp optic white, raw ecru, and delicate botanical lavender frame handcrafted wearable art

**Theme:** light

Ultraviolhat speaks in a luminous, sun-bleached Italian Riviera language: airy optic-white and sea-salt canvases, refined editorial typography, and high-framerate visual pacing where tactile crochet textures take center stage. The interface is deliberately invisible and ethereal: hairline dividers, generous negative space, minimal text chrome, and quiet lavender/warm-sand accents that never compete with the vibrant hues of the handwoven yarns. Typography establishes an effortless Mediterranean elegance through clean, airy geometric proportions and quiet, tracked navigation labels. Components are stripped to the absolute essentials — razor-sharp borders, flat full-bleed hero video and imagery, understated sticky headers, and drawer-based utility panels.

## Tokens — Typography

### Primary Navigation & Body — Jost / Futura PT
- **Substitute:** Futura, Montserrat, Century Gothic
- **Weights:** 300, 400, 500, 600
- **Sizes:** 11px, 12px, 13px, 14px, 16px, 18px
- **Line height:** 1.55 (body), 1.20 (condensed UI)
- **Letter spacing:** 0.08em to 0.16em (tracked uppercase navigation and micro-labels)
- **Role:** Carries brand identity, navigation items, product metadata, pricing, and editorial paragraphs. Balances mid-century geometric rigor with contemporary resort elegance.

### Editorial & Storytelling Display — Editorial Serif / Italian Display (Optional Pairing)
- **Substitute:** Bodoni MT, Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond
- **Weights:** 400, 500
- **Sizes:** 24px, 36px, 52px, 72px
- **Line height:** 1.10–1.20
- **Letter spacing:** -0.01em to 0.02em
- **Role:** Reserved exclusively for capsule titles, artisanal storytelling passages, and founder quotes.

## Components

### Transparent / Sticky Minimal Header
**Role:** Floating navigation bar with seamless background reveal

Full-bleed top bar, 0px border-radius, background translucent Optic Canvas (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`) with backdrop blur. Left section: primary links (`COLLEZIONE`, `RIGUARDO ULTRAVIOLHAT`, `CONTATTI`) in uppercase 12px, letter-spacing 0.12em. Center: lowercase or clean-tracked uppercase brand mark `ULTRAVIOLHAT`. Right: currency/country selector (`Italia | EUR €`), Search, Account, and Cart drawer trigger.

### Full-Bleed Video / Photography Hero
**Role:** Visual hook showcasing crochet movement and seaside light

100vw width, 85vh to 100vh height. Autoplay looping silent video or high-definition editorial shoot. Zero border-radius. Minimalist text overlay or completely unadorned to let the craftsmanship communicate immediately. Bottom anchor chevron or subtle play/pause control.

### Minimalist Product Card
**Role:** High-end boutique product showcase

Optic Canvas (`#ffffff`) card with zero drop shadow. Aspect ratio 3:4 or 4:5 vertical image framing. Smooth secondary image hover transition (swapping from model look to macro crochet texture). Title in 13px uppercase regular, price in Slate Mist (`#757575`) 13px, no badge clutter.

### Slide-Over Cart & Search Drawer
**Role:** Minimal, non-intrusive interaction surfaces

Right-aligned slide-out drawer, 420px max-width, solid Optic Canvas (`#ffffff`) with 1px Hairline Border (`#e8e6e1`) on left edge. Clean line-item listing, single-click quantity adjustment, and full-width checkout button at the bottom.

### Solid Action Button (Checkout / Primary CTA)
**Role:** Confident, high-contrast action trigger

Riviera Ink (`#121212`) solid fill, Optic Canvas (`#ffffff`) text, 0px border radius. Height 48px, horizontal padding 32px. Uppercase 12px, letter-spacing 0.14em. Smooth opacity transition on hover (`opacity: 0.88`).

### Secondary Ghost Button / Text Link
**Role:** Discreet editorial links

No border, no background. Riviera Ink (`#121212`) text with a hairline underline that expands on hover from left to right.

### Global Localization Bar & Footer
**Role:** Quiet, legal, and internationalization hub

Sand Raffia (`#f7f5f2`) or pure white background with 1px top hairline border. Structured link columns (Policies, Terms, Socials, Newsletter subscription). Currency & Language select pills styled with flat borders and minimal down-chevrons.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Prioritize high-resolution macro photography capturing yarn weaves, metallic thread flecks, and crochet texture
- Keep all structural corners razor-sharp (0px border radius across buttons, cards, and inputs)
- Maintain ample negative space (minimum 96px vertical section spacing on desktop)
- Keep typography tracked, light, and airy — especially uppercase category headings and nav items
- Let the vibrant natural colors of the products (ocean teals, sun yellows, terracotta) provide the chromatic richness against a calm neutral canvas

### Don't
- Do not apply drop shadows, rounded corners, or decorative gradients
- Do not clutter the header or hero banner with promotional popups, badge banners, or countdown timers
- Do not use loud primary colors for UI buttons; keep UI controls monochrome (`#121212` and `#ffffff`)
- Do not compress line spacing or letter spacing on navigation links
